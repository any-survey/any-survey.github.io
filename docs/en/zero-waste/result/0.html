Tips for your next step

If you are already doing well, the next step is not to become stricter on your own but to bring more people along. Suggest a small activity at work or in your neighborhood, such as a container-sharing day or a second-hand swap. Writing down your know-how and passing it on gives hesitant beginners a friendly guide. And on days when you feel worn out, it is fine to take a break, because keeping going for the long run is what counts most. Remember, too, that your habits may be quietly rubbing off on the people around you.

Ideas by area of daily life

When you shop, look for stores that sell loose items or use little packaging, and in the kitchen plan meals around leftover ingredients to cut food waste. In the bathroom, favor long-lasting tools and refills, and in the closet think of mending and passing things on first. Make public transport and walking your default for getting around, and tidy unused subscriptions and notifications online. It is a small step, but a real one. Simply keeping up what you already do is more than enough, with no need to keep adding more.
