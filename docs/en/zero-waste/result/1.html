Your practice level

You answered 'mostly' more often than 'always'. Your direction is already set, and you are handling a good share of daily life well. If a few boxes are empty, it is more likely that circumstances got in the way than that you lack the will. On busy days the travel cup gets forgotten, and on rushed days takeout wins. Everyone has gaps like that, and being able to see where they are is exactly the advantage of this stage.

Your life right now

On a normal day you bring your own bag and cup and sort your recycling faithfully, but when an outing runs long or the schedule gets tight, a disposable item can slip in. There is a bit of a gap between the days you are set on it and the days you are not. Even so, knowing which kind of day is hard for you is a real strength. You probably already make plenty of eco-minded choices without a second thought. That sense of balance makes it easier to keep going for a long time.

Tips for your next step

The easiest way to close the gaps is to keep your gear where you can see it. Leave a foldable bag and a small cup in your everyday bag, and gather reusable containers near the front door. Rather than changing everything at once, pick just one more habit this month and move to the next once it feels natural; that pace lasts. Doing it with a friend helps, since you can remind each other when one of you forgets.

Ideas by area of daily life

When you shop, add one product at a time that comes in paper or glass instead of plastic packaging. In the kitchen, try reusable containers and cloth covers in place of cling film and plastic bags, and make refilling detergent and shower products a regular routine. Pass on or mend clothes you no longer wear, and switch short trips to walking or public transport. E-receipts and inbox cleanups are easy extras too. You do not have to change every item at once; whichever one catches your eye is plenty.
