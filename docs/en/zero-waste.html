Trash

The amount of waste we produce keeps growing every year. All this waste pollutes the environment and makes problems like global warming worse. Disposing of waste uses a great deal of energy and can cause problems such as groundwater contamination. Reducing the waste we produce is essential to solving these problems. To do that, we need to use fewer disposable items and actively practice recycling and reuse. Protecting the environment is no different from protecting human health. If all of us cut down on waste, use fewer disposable items, and join in recycling, we can protect both the planet and our own health.

Plastic

Global plastic production was under 2 million tons in the 1950s, but it reached 320 million tons in 2021. That is more than a 160-fold increase in 70 years, and about 8 million tons of plastic flow into the ocean every year, threatening ecosystems and human health. Recycling plastic into new plastic reduces the need for new plastic production. Using less plastic also saves energy and resources, so you can expect economic benefits as well. Please use a reusable shopping bag or eco bag, use a tumbler instead of disposable or paper cups, and reuse water bottles and beverage containers. When small efforts like these add up, we can greatly cut plastic use and make a real difference in protecting the planet.

Disposable cup

Using disposable cups has a serious impact on the global environment. Plastic cups do not break down naturally, so they stay on the earth for years and pollute water and soil. On top of that, producing disposable cups takes a lot of energy and resources at great cost, which speeds up environmental destruction and climate change. Using reusable cups instead of disposable ones is good for the environment. Making a reusable cup costs less than the resources and energy that disposable cups require, so there are economic benefits too. Join in protecting the environment by switching to a reusable cup.

Data trash

Our daily lives are full of data. But did you know that this data has a big impact on the environment? Running data centers uses a lot of electricity, and making devices such as servers and smartphones takes a lot of resources. Disposing of the devices used to store data is also a problem. There is plenty we can do, too! When using a smartphone or computer, we can use a screen saver or power-saving mode to cut unnecessary energy use. We can keep only the important data and sort out or delete the rest to reduce the storage we use. It also matters to dispose of electronics separately from general waste. What matters is thinking about how the data we consume affects the planet, and finding ways to act on it!

Fashion pollution

Clothing is one of the necessities of daily life, but making and throwing it away has a big impact on the environment. Producing clothes takes a lot of energy, water, and chemicals, which can pollute the air, water, and other parts of the natural environment. Disposal causes problems too. Clothes have a low recycling rate, so a huge amount of clothing waste is generated, and it harms the natural environment. We can enjoy stylish clothes while reducing pollution. Choose good-quality clothes that you can wear for longer. The longer you wear your clothes, the less needs to be produced, which greatly helps protect nature. Recycle or donate your clothes! When clothes are reused, less energy and fewer resources are needed to make new ones, which helps protect the environment. Dispose of clothes the right way! Clothes should go into recyclable waste rather than general waste, following your local recycling rules. Even simple choices and actions can do a lot to protect the environment.

Fossil fuel

Fossil fuels are one of the biggest culprits of environmental pollution. Burning fossil fuels releases large amounts of greenhouse gases and harmful substances. This leads to problems such as global warming, air pollution, and acid rain. But there is a lot we can do to reduce the pollution caused by fossil fuels. One is to save energy and use eco-friendly alternative energy. We can also cut air pollution by choosing eco-friendly transportation, such as public transit or a bicycle. Even our simple choices and actions can do a lot for the environment.
