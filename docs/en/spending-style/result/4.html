Spending Style

Save day-to-day, then splurge on the big moment

save then splurgeexperientialistimpulsive

Shopper, you save diligently day-to-day and then go all-in for the moments that matter. Small everyday expenses? Easy to skip. But when something you really want appears, there's no hesitation. The 'big splurge for me' is itself the motivation to keep saving. To keep 'special moments' from arriving too often, planning your big-spend events for the year in advance keeps things balanced.

Spending profile

The Splurge Saver brings together save, impulse and experience. Day to day it is used to cutting back, yet when a moment moves it deeply it spends big regardless of plan, giving it two faces. Small outlays are guarded carefully while big emotional moments get generous spending, so the yardstick is the size of the feeling rather than the amount. Afterward it usually convinces itself that the moment was worth it.

How you spend

On weekdays this is the thrifty person who carries a tumbler, packs lunch and deletes the delivery app. Then news of a favorite singer’s concert, a loved one’s anniversary or a long-dreamed-of destination catches the eye and a large payment suddenly goes through. Right after paying comes a mix of thrill and a little worry, and a few days later it slips back into saving mode to even things out. Because the swing repeats, the shape of monthly spending tends to be uneven.

Spending habit tips

Since big spending arrives suddenly, setting aside a little at a time for the splurge makes the moment of payment feel much lighter. A simple method such as dividing accounts by purpose is enough. Writing down a few moments for which there will be no skimping makes it easier to tell an impulsive large payment from a truly meaningful one. After a big payment, a recovery rule of trimming next month a bit works better than self-blame.

Best spending mates

The best matches for the Splurge Saver are the Frugal Experiencer and the Yolo Flexer. The Frugal Experiencer understands the everyday thrift deeply and shows how to trim the cost of experiences with a plan. The Yolo Flexer joins in at the moment a splurge is called for and adds the confidence that spending now is right. One gives balance and the other gives cheering, so even a big payment feels easier.
