Spending Style

Thrifty by default, but a great deal grabs me

deal huntervalue impulsethrifty

Shopper, you're thrifty by habit but your hand moves first when a great-value deal appears. You resist unnecessary spending, yet you never miss a practical item that screams 'a steal at this price!' The very knack for spotting a good deal becomes a joy of saving. To keep 'bought it because it was cheap' items from piling up, pausing once to ask 'do I really need this' makes your thrift even sharper.

Spending profile

The Deal Hunter combines save, impulse and practical. Day to day it is wary of unnecessary spending, but a signal that a price has dropped turns even an unplanned item into now is the chance. The drop itself becomes the reason to buy, and how much was saved makes up a big share of the satisfaction. The thrill of a successful hunt sits at the center of its motivation to spend.

How you spend

Several coupon and rewards apps sit on the phone, and a flash-sale alert makes it stop whatever it is doing to check. A big discount number catches the eye first, and adding one more item to hit a coupon threshold is common. Something that would not have been bought at full price sometimes gets bought on sale and then sits in a drawer. On the other hand, it is excellent at catching the moment when something it already uses drops in price and swapping it in thriftily.

Spending habit tips

The single most effective habit is asking before paying whether the item would be bought at full price. Putting tempting items on a wishlist and waiting a couple of days leaves only what is truly needed. Keep alerts only for the apps that matter, and once a month look back at what was bought thanks to discounts to see whether it is actually used. Counting the amount really spent instead of the amount saved makes the hunting sharper still.

Best spending mates

The best matches for the Deal Hunter are the Value Master and the Stockpile Shopper. The Value Master has clear criteria for telling a truly cheap item from one that only looks cheap, and steadies the hunter when a discount is pulling hard. The Stockpile Shopper is a good partner for splitting or jointly buying bulk deals, making one sale go further. Sharing the same practical taste, the two also trade information often.
