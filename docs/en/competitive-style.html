My Competitive Style

What is the competitive style test?

A round of a game, a clash of ideas in a meeting, a bet with friends — we live inside competition big and small every day. This is a light psychology test that reflects how you try to win in those moments. Across who you win with (alone vs. together), how you win (strategy vs. drive), and how badly you want it (win-hungry vs. easygoing), your own competitive style emerges. Which type you get? You will only know at the very end. Light and fun, just like MBTI.

Competition reveals the real you

Traits you never noticed sharpen the moment a contest begins. Some people quietly calculate the board alone; others break straight through on sheer momentum. Some are not satisfied until they win; for others, enjoying the process comes first. There is no right answer — every competitive style has its own strength. The more honestly you answer, the more you meet the real shape of your drive to win. (This test is for fun.)

How it works

Answer 9 simple questions while picturing yourself in everyday competitive moments. It takes about a minute. At the end you will meet your competitive style with a detailed read, and you can share the result to compare styles with friends. So — which type is waiting for you?
