The quiet handy problem-solver

Dog is a cool, handy problem-solver! Sorting things out alone, without a fuss, and staying easygoing no matter what. Dog is the dependable one you can count on.

Personality traits

The ISTP dog is a quiet fixer built from Introversion (I), Sensing (S), Thinking (T) and Perceiving (P). It says little but solves the problem in front of it by touching and rolling it around. It ignores unnecessary fuss and then moves like lightning when it counts, and though its affection is understated it sprawls comfortably beside someone it trusts. It has a quiet talent for figuring out how a new tool or toy works.

Walks & play

Walks tend to be short and to the point, efficient and only as long as needed. It strolls at ease, then bursts into speed when something interesting appears and checks it out in person. It likes challenge play it can crack alone over interactive games, so it zeroes in on treat-stuffed toys, opening doors and retrieving things from boxes. With other dogs it mingles lightly only when needed and soon returns to its own activity.

Living together

Its self-reliance means anything it can open, such as door handles, drawers and trash cans, may get opened, so watch the safety latches. Giving it a chance to use its dexterity once a day with a food puzzle or sniffing game reduces problem behavior. Training suits a short demonstration and then letting it try over long explanations. Do not force affection; accept it when it approaches on its own and trust deepens.

Best buddies

The fearless ESTP dog draws the quiet ISTP into thrilling adventures as an active partner. The curious INTP dog explores on its own too, so they keep a comfortable distance without getting in each other’s way, and the earnest ISTJ dog respects the ISTP’s independence and keeps it silent company. A friend who meddles or constantly demands attention is the kind the ISTP tries to avoid.
