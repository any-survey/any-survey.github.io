The steady, earnest honor pup

Dog is a steady, earnest honor pup! Always keeping to a routine once it's set, and handling every duty without a slip. Dog is as dependable and solid as they come.

Personality traits

The ISTJ dog is a diligent model student made of Introversion (I), Sensing (S), Thinking (T) and Judging (J). It finds security in repetition: meals at the same hour and walks along the same course, and once it has learned a rule it rarely breaks it. It does not display emotions flamboyantly, yet its sense of responsibility to finish what it takes on is strong. In unfamiliar situations it checks things one at a time and takes them in slowly.

Walks & play

Its walks run like clockwork, and at the appointed time it may already be waiting in front of the leash. It treasures the ritual of checking the same tree and the same corner in order along the usual route. In play, it finds more satisfaction in games with clear rules and goals, such as fetch or sit-and-wait, than in free-form variety. It tends to keep its favorite toys in place and may even carry one back to its spot when play ends.

Living together

A consistent environment is its best tranquilizer. Keep mealtimes, walking routes and the bed’s position steady where you can, and when something must change, change it one item at a time rather than all at once. It masters training best when steps are clear and repeatable, and it remembers what it learns for a long time. A wrongly learned habit is hard to undo, though, so use accurate cues from the start. When change is needed, a preparation signal in advance helps.

Best buddies

The ESTJ dog, another routine type, has a similar daily rhythm and sense of rules, so they keep a comfortable distance from each other. The devoted ISFJ dog respects the ISTJ’s habits and becomes a dependable walking companion, and the surprisingly curious ENFP dog adds a little vitality to its firm routine. A friend given to impulsive outbursts will not match its rhythm, so first meetings are best kept short.
