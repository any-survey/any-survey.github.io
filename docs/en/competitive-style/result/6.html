My Competitive Style

The Captain

Let's go! Fire everyone up and win together

fired-up captainmorale boosterwin together

Competitor, you're the Captain who fires up the team's morale and wins together. One "Let's go!" reheats a slumping team — you're the living center of energy. You don't swallow the will to win alone; you share it and pull the whole team into the fight. In the hardest moments you're out front shouting, and so the team never gives up. Once in a while, listen for the quiet teammate too — heat plus care is unbeatable.
