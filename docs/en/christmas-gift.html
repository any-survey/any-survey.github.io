My Christmas Gift Style

What is the Christmas gift style test?

The way you choose a gift says a lot about who you are. This light Christmas personality quiz looks at what you value when giving (usefulness vs. sentiment), how you prepare (well ahead vs. in the moment), and what form your gift takes (something to keep vs. time spent together) — then reveals what kind of Santa you are this year. Like a wrapped present, the result stays a secret until the very end!

Every gift has the shape of a heart

Even on the same Christmas, everyone prepares gifts differently. Some think it through carefully, some follow their heart, and some give a day together instead of a thing. There is no right answer to which gift is better. But once you know how you express your heart, picking presents gets a lot more fun — and you may see the gift tastes of the people you love in a new light. (This test is just for fun.)

How it works

In 9 gift-related situations, simply pick the option that feels more like you. It takes about a minute. At the end you will unwrap your gift style along with a small tip to make this year’s gifting more fun. Share the result with friends and compare your styles — it might make this year’s gift hunt a little easier!
