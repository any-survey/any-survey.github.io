Into the wild! A cave

You're worn out by everyday life! Do you want to be free from everything in your daily routine? How about a cave where you can focus only on yourself? Lighten up and take a deep breath. The world is far too beautiful to live alone in a cave!

Why this home suits you

Of the five traits, the sensitivity that tires quickly under outside stimulation came out highest. A day full of people, noise and notifications drains you faster than most, so a snug space cut off from the outside is the surest way to recover. You also take responsibilities seriously, which makes burning out easier. A cave here is less a place to run away to than a charging station where you gather your strength again.

A day in this home

You keep alarms to a minimum and start quietly in the dim light as daylight seeps in slowly. The center of the day is a long stretch of absorption in one chosen task, with the phone placed out of reach so nothing breaks in. Lunch is kept simple, and in the afternoon a short breath of outside air clears the head. At night you sink into whatever you love under soft light. Cutting off completely can weigh on the mind, so keep a thread to others with short messages.

Tips for styling your space

The key is controlling sound and light yourself. Heavy curtains, rugs and fabric furniture absorb noise, and several low, indirect lamps are kinder to the eyes than a ceiling light. Separate the place where you focus from the place where you lie down, and give the phone one charging spot where it stays. If you miss nature, add a small plant or the soft sound of water. Set a fixed time for airing the room so the air never stagnates.

What to check when choosing

The cozier the space, the more likely it gets little daylight and collects moisture, so check ventilation, humidity and any signs of mold first. If almost no sun comes in, you will need to plan time in bright places during the day. Make sure the entrance and emergency exits are clear, and listen for how much you hear from the floor above and the neighbors. Since time alone may stretch on, also confirm that you can reach someone right away when you need to.
