Close to work! A studio apartment

You live each day diligently for a wonderful life! Your pride in your work and your best effort are impressive! For you, who keep improving yourself even at home, how about a studio with little to worry about, where you can focus even more on yourself?

Why this home suits you

Of the five traits, the diligence to plan and follow through came out highest. You do not mind meeting people but are careful about how much energy relationships take, so you meet when needed and otherwise move efficiently. A tendency to worry also means you feel secure in a small space you can control. The wish to cut commuting time and spend what is left on growing yourself led to a studio close to work.

A day in this home

Your morning is relaxed because getting ready was finished the night before. The time saved by walking to work goes to exercise or study, and you might drop by home at lunch to rest. After work you focus on self-improvement, such as a certification or a hobby, at a tidy desk. At night you jot down tomorrow’s tasks and go to bed. When work is close, the line between work and rest blurs easily, so put your work tools out of sight once the day is over.

Tips for styling your space

In a small space, dividing zones is the key. Marking the desk area and the rest area with a rug or lighting makes it easier to switch between work and rest. Keep two kinds of light: a bright desk lamp for focus and a warm mood lamp for unwinding. Stack storage upward and use cabinets with doors to clear things from sight, and set a tidying rule so your diligent side can shine. Even a single plant on the desk gives a plain room some breathing space.

What to check when choosing

Since the point is living near work, walk the real commute once in the morning and once in the evening. Building security and access control, how parcels are received, and how much noise comes through floors and walls all matter. Check ventilation, window direction and daylight, and whether storage is enough. When you sign, confirm how the building is managed, what costs are included and the contract terms in writing, which cuts down on worries later.
